About

A few details about this app.

Daymark

Daymark is a simple, minimalist productivity app designed to help you organize your tasks, manage deadlines, and keep track of your progress. With a clean interface and a calm, distraction-free design, Daymark helps you focus on what matters most, one task at a time.

Your tasks are stored locally in your browser, so your data stays on your device. Daymark does not automatically sync tasks between devices.
